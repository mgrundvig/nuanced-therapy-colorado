Melanie Grundvig, MFTC, MA

Hi. I'm Melanie. I became a therapist because I'm deeply interested in people and the human experience. I care about providing a nuanced look at my clients so that they feel truly seen and heard. My style is warm and inquisitive. With me you'll answer questions you've never been asked, feel safe and cared for, and be challenged to move towards the hard things you're capable of facing.

Background & credentials

  • M.A. in Marriage and Family Therapy
  • 5 years of clinical experience working with couples and individuals
  • Training in EFT, Gottman Couples Therapy, Internal Family Systems Therapy, and Existential Therapy

Outside the therapy room

I'm a new mom, a philosophy nerd, and mediocre at all things outdoors. On the weekends you can catch me towing my daughter in a bike trailer, snuggled at home reading a book, or most often, managing whatever new chaos a baby brings.
