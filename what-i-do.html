Frequently asked questions

What should I expect in a free consult?

The free consult is a low pressure space for me to meet with prospective clients and determine fit. I get to see if my expertise matches your needs, and you get to decide if I feel like the right therapist for you. No expectations or catches.

How often will we meet?

Frequency is determined by my clinical expertise and client choice. After our first session, we decide on cadence together. Oftentimes when beginning therapy, it is most effective to meet weekly.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes.

Do you accept insurance?

At this time I do not accept insurance. This allows for increased privacy with no third-party payment system, as well as the ability to work with a broader range of diagnoses or non-diagnosed clients whose care is excluded from insurance coverage.
